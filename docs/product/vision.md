# Product vision

## The problem

Selection processes are a skill. Reading a job description critically, understanding how an ATS filters resumes, knowing what to study for a role, and reasoning through an interview are all learnable. That knowledge has always been hard to keep alive: it is fragmented across people, rarely written down, and forgotten between job searches.

AI made this worse in a subtle way. It is now trivial to generate a resume, a cover letter, or an interview answer without understanding any of it. The output gets better while the person's own understanding gets weaker. Companies feel the other side of this: candidates who present well on paper and cannot reason about their own experience.

## The goal

Build a community that expands its knowledge of selection processes, even in the age of AI, when keeping that knowledge alive in one's mind has become difficult. Help Me Get Hired is a platform for knowledge and logical thinking that helps candidates **and** companies keep the spirit of intelligent engineering alive.

## Principles

1. **AI explains, it does not replace.** Every AI output comes with the reasoning: why the ATS score is 6, why this experience matters for this role, why this topic is in the study plan. The candidate should be able to reproduce the reasoning without the tool.
2. **Sequential, deliberate progress.** The journey is a pipeline. Each step builds on the previous one and the candidate sees each step complete before the next begins. No shortcuts to the end result.
3. **Grounded in the candidate's real data.** Analyses are built from the candidate's own experiences, projects, and profile, retrieved through RAG. The platform does not invent experience.
4. **Knowledge stays in the community.** Learnings, study plans, and interview patterns are meant to be shared and refined, not locked in one person's session.
5. **Open by default.** The project is public. Decisions are written down. Anyone can propose work.

## Who it serves

| Audience | What they get |
| --- | --- |
| Candidates | A guided preparation journey for a specific job: profile analysis, ATS score, resume rebuild, study plan, cover letter, mock interview, and a summary of readiness. |
| Companies | A community of candidates who understand their own experience and can reason about it, and a shared reference for what good selection processes look like. |
| Contributors | An open codebase with a clear stack, written decisions, and refined tasks to pick up. |

## What success looks like

- A candidate finishes the journey for a job and can explain, unaided, why their resume and preparation changed.
- The ATS score and recommendations are consistent enough that candidates trust them across different job descriptions.
- Contributors can go from reading the docs to shipping a refined task without asking how the project works.

## Out of scope for now

- Multiple simultaneous profiles or uploads per user.
- Applying to jobs on the candidate's behalf.
- Recruiter-facing tooling. Companies participate through the community, not through a separate product surface, until that need is proven.
